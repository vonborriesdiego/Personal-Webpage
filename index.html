About Me

I'm Diego Andrés von Borries Flores. I was born in Bolivia, but I have a strong connection with two countries that I love, Mexico and Brazil, the reason I'll explain you below.

I'm a biomedical engineer who got passionated about the field of statistics and its junction with computer science, merging into a fascinating field that we know as data science. My journey took place back in 2015 when my path into the engineering world begun. I was accepted to be part of the biomedical engineering program at Universidad Politécnica de Pachuca, located in the city of Pachuca the capital of Hidalgo, one of the most relevant states from Mexico. The reason why I chose biomedical engineering was because I considered; and still considering, biomedical engineering as a game changer, a major that blends the field of engineering with the field of medicine, providing the professionals with a wide range of knowledge from multiple domains expanding the opportinity to act in multiple areas.

Back in my years as an undergraduate student, from very early on my career I could experience firsthand the duties of a biomedical engineer by taking three different internships. The first internship consisted of 200 hrs of service supporting the maintenance division of a nephrology clinic called Centro Integral de Nefrología located in Pachuca, there I gained knowledge on the functionality of hemodialysis machine and the proper way to provide preventive and corrective maintenance.

The second internship was at Hospital de General de Zona Médico Familiar Nº1, a public hospital located also in Pachuca. It was designed to provide biomedical engineering majors a broader experience as engineer by not just focusing on equipments of a single specific area, but to work in a wide variety of medical equipment going from a simple vital signs monitor to an X-ray machine and supporting the tomography experts with the maintenance sessions. This was a longer internship of 600 hrs, enough time to gained knowledge of the different devices as well as the multiple areas involving the hospital.

As a last internship, I conducted a project based on providing corrective maintenance services to seven obstetric ultrasound located at Hospital General de Zona Nº47 from Mexico city. The project was developed as part of the Service-Learning capstone program, a culminating undergraduate project required for the completion of the biomedical engineering degree. This type of graduation offers the students the possibility to apply their academic knowledge to solve real-world problems and fulfill a specific need for a community partner. This was the longest service I offered as a biomedical engineer providing corrective maintenance, the program requested to fulfill a 900 hrs of service in order to conclude the graduation. Once I fulfill the requirement and noticed that my work was benefical for the community, I felt some relief knowing that my profession had the possibility to contribute with the wealth of the society, but I also felt that dedicating my life to provide maintenance service wasn't enough in order to make a huge impact on society.

During this meditation moments I had after accomplishing with my biomedical degree, I realized that in order to contribute with the wealth of the people and with the medical community I had to go deeper and gather more knowledge and expertise in different fields. I also noticed that during my formation I never felt that much excited while applying my knowledge to provide maintenance services, but I had a lot of curiosity about software and the junction between software and hardware that allowed the development of robotic projects. In my last year as a biomedical engineer major I took many disciplines related with programming languages, coding, and statistics. It was at this exact moment that my journey into statistics and data science begun.

My Path into Data Science

After concluding my degree in 2019, I kept wondering how should I proceed in order to gain more knowledge. I was convinced that I must pursue a master degree, but questions like what type of master program, in which field, and must importantly; where, kept bumping into my head. In the meanwhile, I decided to take what Coursera called a 'Data Science Specialization' offered by IBM and that was the turning point of my career. It was a specialization that didn't go deeper with the analysis and tools, but it made me realize that data science was the right path to follow.

In 2022 I was accepted to be part of a research internship that took place at the biomechatronic lab from the polytechnique school at Universidade de São Paulo. It was my first experience related with research and science. I supported a team of experts and doctoral students conduct a series of investigations based on sleep deprivation, the research I joined was based on measuring the reaction time of patients with sleep deprivation in order to study how lack of sleep potentially affect the motor responses of the patients. This research was not inmersed to deep into data science, but to me was fascinating have collaborated in a high quality research. My dutties relied on supporting with the development of the user interface application used during the sessions for the patients in order to accomplish their tasks, it was developed in C++, also I supperted the team with the collection of biological signals.

During my period as internship researcher for the biomechatronic lab, I was selected to be part of a prestigious extension group called Skyrats , an extension group (mostly for undergrads), focused to research and develop autonomous intelligent drones. A fantastic extension group where I learned a lot about hardware focused to enhence the performace of the drone, the mechanics involved behind these fascinating devices, as well as all the software development process in order for the drone to accomplish different computer vision taks. I was accepted to be part of the software team, there I could improve my knowledge in Python, C++, and learned about Robotic Operating System or ROS. Additionally, I had the chance participate together with members of the team in the ABII Challenge, a national technological competition which took place in Joinville - Santa Catarina, where we compited on the drone category and got the first place. I'm still being a memeber of the group, a veteran one as the new ones call us. It's a fantastic group of talented student involving multiples areas going from different engineering majors to major students outside engineering like business administration, industry design, marketing, etc. They accept anyone who has the curiosity to learn about these fascinating devices and contribute with the team.

A year after (2023), I took my second research internship where I finally dived deeper into the world of statistics and data science. This internship was conducted at the Signal Processing Lab from the electronic and computer department from Escuela de Engenharia de São Carlos. There it could work with Bayesian inference and causal network related with the detection of the factors/causes that triggers the three different types of sleep apneas. It was a fantastic experience where I could shared work with relevant researchers and professor from this prestigious institution.

Thanks to this internship and my contributions I was accepted to the master program in public health from the medical faculty Faculdade de Medicina de Ribeirão Preto - Universidade de São Paulo in 2025. Since then, I've been applying my knoweledge gathered thorugh the years to contribute with state of the art data analysis techniques to the study of cancer. After a challenging procedure applying survival analysis to analyze cancer data provided by the National Institute of Cancer (INCA) from Brazil using semiparametric transformation methods with a Bayesian approach, on Agust 14th I presented my results to a committe of respected professors as part of the Qualification Exam requested by the program, and as a result of the quality of my findings and the impact of the research I was selected to continue my research through a direct-entry doctoral program. After all I've been through, this moment made me realize that I'm in the right way to accomplish my goal on contributing with the society. I accepted the invitantion and right now I'm fulfilling all the requirements to become a doctoral student.

This have been my journey until the day I wrote this, a crazy and internation journey that motivates me to keep growing as a professional and as a human being. Thanks for taking the time to read all my professional path that thrills me any time I have the chance to share it with someone!

Services

Statistical Analysis & Hypothesis Testing

Application of rigorous descriptive and inferential statistics methods conducted in Python to uncover meaningful patterns hidden in complex datasets, including exploratory data analysis to understand distributions, correlations, and outliers, as well as formal hypothesis testing to validate assumptions and support data-driven decision.

Machine Learning

Implementation of machine learning techniques, such as regression, classification, and ensemble methods (e.g., random forests, gradient boosting), and neural networks to produce robust predictive models capable of handling complex, high-dimensional datasets and supporting data-driven forecasting and decision-making.

Bayesian Modeling & Biostatistics

Application of Bayesian methodologies to classic statistical approaches in order to enhance the quality of the results. Bayesian framework has demonstrated its capabilities to success in scenarios where classical approaches provide unreliable results due to their strict assumptions or the lack of large datasets. I recently presented a study blending Bayesian framework with semiparametric transformation models to determine prognostic factors in cancer patients.

Data Engineering & Pipelines

Design and implementation of ETL pipelines for the extraction, cleaning, and transformation of raw, unstructured datasets, ensuring data quality and consistency to support downstream statistical analysis and machine learning workflows.

Dashboards & Data Visualization

Development of interactive dashboards and visual reporting tools using Plotly and Power BI, translating complex statistical and predictive model outputs into clear, accessible visuals that support monitoring, exploration, and data-driven decision-making for technical and non-technical stakeholders alike.

Consulting & Technical Reporting

Translation of statistical and machine learning results into clear technical reports and actionable recommendations for research or business decision-making, along with private courses and mentoring in statistics, Python, and data science for individuals and teams looking to build their analytical skills.

Projects

Semiparametric Transformation Models in Survival Data Analysis in Presence of Censored Data and Covariates

The project focused on the implementation of a hierarchical Bayesian framework to three semiparametric transformation models, the analysis was based on a dataset of 862 cancer patients provided by the National Cancer Institute of Cancer (INCA) from Rio de Janeiro, Brazil. The purpose of the analysis was to determine prognostic factors that increased the risk to have of cancer.

Review Project

Traffic Accidents and Impacts on Public Health: An analysis of data from the state of São Paulo, Brazil, from 2019 to 2024, under a Bayesian approach

Analysis conducted under a Bayesian approach applied to a traffic accident dataset of 645 minincipalities by year from the state of São Paulo, Brazil. The aim of this analysis was to find relevant insights from the dataset in order to promote the severities a trafic accident could reach, as well as the age group frequently involved in these situations.

Review Project

Outcomes and Mortality Predictors on Pediatric Candidemia: A single-center experience from Brazil.

Conducted an analysis applying survival analysis techniques, such as the proportional hazards regression model proposed by Cox to analyze 102 pediatric patients, under the age of 18, that had experienced episodes of candidemia fungos. The analysis intended to describe and highlight the factors that increased the risk of have from candidemia.

Review Project

Bayesian Logistic Regression: A comparative analysis between the classic logistic regression model with a Bayesian logistic regression model

The present analysis focused to highlight the differences between the two approaches and determine the advantages of the Bayesian approach to enhance the analysis, resulting in more reliable outcomes. To conduct this analysis, the candidemia dataset was proposed, the analysis revealed the strengths of the Bayesian approach, producing relevant findings while the classical approach struggle to provide feasibles outcomes, presenting wide confidence intervals.
